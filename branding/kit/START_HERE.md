# ScrapSheet Brand Kit

Open `06_Guidelines/ScrapSheet_Brand_Overview.png` for a visual overview and `06_Guidelines/ScrapSheet_Brand_Guide.pdf` for usage guidance.

## Contents
- 01_Logos: horizontal, stacked, standalone wordmarks; SVG, transparent PNG, vector PDF. Primary, all-teal, black, and white versions.
- 02_Icons: filled, outline, and monochrome transparent marks; cream/mint full-bleed 1024 app artwork.
- 03_Social: avatars, a cover, a square promotional graphic, and a web sharing card.
- 04_Email: HTML signature and accompanying image.
- 05_Print: front/back business cards with bleed and US Letter letterhead; SVG, PNG, PDF.
- 06_Guidelines: visual overview and six-page brand guide.
- 07_Web: favicon SVG/ICO, PNG icon sizes, Apple touch icon, CSS tokens, and head markup.

## Website lettering
The wordmark is vector artwork reconstructed from the supplied website screenshot, not newly typed text. The original font family was not provided or identified. The screenshot is low-resolution; this reconstruction may differ slightly from an original vector source. Keep the existing website font in the app. Supporting material uses portable Arial/system sans fallbacks or embedded Open Sans in the guide. No font installation is required to use the outlined logos.

## Icon design
The softer notepad uses clean editable geometry based on the approved flat concept, including three binding strokes and two note strokes. Teal #247F82, cream #F5F2EB, pale mint #DDF5EE, ink #1C1C1C. These are standardized palette selections, not sampled production specifications.

## Print production
Business card trim is 3.5 x 2 in with 0.125 in bleed. PDF page size is 3.75 x 2.25 in; trim and bleed boxes are embedded. Letterhead is US Letter. PDFs use RGB vector artwork, not a printer-specific CMYK or PDF/X profile. Request conversion using the print shop's intended stock and profile. PNGs are digital previews, not the preferred print masters. Do not add rounded masks to full-bleed store artwork; platforms apply their own shape.

## Email setup
Open email-signature.html next to signature-logo.png to preview. Insert the image through your mail client or host signature-logo.png at your own HTTPS address and replace the relative src before deployment. Local image paths do not travel reliably with sent email. The website URL uses scrap.sector809labs.com. No personal contact fields are invented.

## Publishing
These are prepared assets; no website, email account, or public profile has been changed. Review contact details and production settings before use. The folder may be unzipped and handed directly to a designer or developer.
