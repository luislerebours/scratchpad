# Google Play listing kit

Everything for the store listing of the Android app
(`com.sector809labs.scrapsheet`). Images are opaque PNGs at the sizes Play
asks for, made from the live app with a demo account and made-up content
(`maya.lee@example.com`, a Lisbon trip, drawn test pictures).

## Text

**App name** (25 / 30): ScrapSheet: Notes & Files

**Short description** (60 / 80): Move text, photos and files between your devices in seconds.

**Full description** (paste as is; under 4,000 characters):

```
ScrapSheet is the fastest way to move text, photos and files between your phone, tablet and computer. Drop something in on one device and it's already on the next.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Type or paste notes, links and photos into a sheet. They sync instantly to every device you're signed in on.
• Keep files right next to your notes: PDFs, photos, documents, anything up to 100 MB (Pro).
• Share to ScrapSheet from any app with Android's Share menu.
• Send a link instead of an attachment: share a file or a whole sheet with a link that expires in 1, 7 or 30 days (Pro).
• Search every sheet and file name, right on your device.
• Light or dark, your call.
• Download everything as a .zip whenever you like.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything you put in ScrapSheet (text, images, files and their names) is end-to-end encrypted with AES-256 on your device before it's sent. We can't read it, and neither can anyone who gets hold of our servers. No ads. No tracking.

FREE AND PRO
Free: 1 sheet and 1 file up to 10 MB, on all your devices.
Pro: 10 sheets, 5 GB of storage, files up to 100 MB, and share links. Monthly or yearly, billed through Google Play. Cancel anytime in the Play Store.

Your sheets are on the web too, at scrap.sector809labs.com.

Questions? support@sector809labs.com
```
