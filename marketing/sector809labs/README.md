# ScrapSheet: marketing kit for sector809labs.com

This kit is for the session that maintains **sector809labs.com**. It adds
ScrapSheet as a Sector 809 Labs product:

1. **A product card** on the products list (or home page), linking to the
   ScrapSheet page.
2. **A ScrapSheet page** (suggested path: `/products/scrapsheet` or
   `/scrapsheet`, whichever matches the site) that explains the app and sends
   people to it.

Everything below is final copy, ready to use. Fit it into the site's existing
layout, type and components. Where this kit and the site's own design system
disagree, follow the site and keep ScrapSheet's colors only for ScrapSheet
accents (see "Brand").

## Rules for this kit

- **No prices, no plan names.** Don't mention Free, Pro, dollar amounts or
  trials anywhere. The app shows its own plans after people sign up.
- **Android is "coming soon".** No Google Play badge and no Play link yet.
  Use only the line given below.
- **The main button always goes to the web app:**
  https://scrap.sector809labs.com
- **Use only the flat logos in `assets/logos/`.** The ScrapSheet 3D app icon is
  only for home screens and app stores, so don't use it on the website.
- **Keep the privacy claims exactly as written.** They're true to how the app
  works. Don't strengthen them (for example, don't write "military-grade",
  "unhackable" or "zero-knowledge").
- **The screenshots use a demo account with made-up notes and files.** That's
  fine to show as is. The Lisbon photo in them is real and needs a credit on
  the page (see "Photo credit").
- **Photo credit is required.** Put the credit line from "Photo credit" in
  small type near the hero, or in the page footer.

### How it works (3 steps)

1. **Drop it in.** Type, paste a link or a photo, or attach a file on any
   device.
2. **It syncs instantly.** Every device you're signed in on gets it right away.
3. **Pull it up anywhere.** Copy it, download it or keep working on your next
   device.

### Private by design

- **Title:** Private by design
- **Text:** Everything you put in ScrapSheet (text, images, files and their
  names) is end-to-end encrypted with AES-256 on your device before it's sent.
  Your password never leaves your device. We can't read your sheets, and
  neither can anyone who gets hold of our servers.
- **Points:**
  - No ads. No tracking.
  - Download everything as a .zip whenever you like.
  - Delete your account and everything in it, anytime.
- **Link:** Read the privacy policy → https://scrap.sector809labs.com/privacy

### Everywhere you are

- **Title:** On every device you use

### FAQ

**What is ScrapSheet?**
A shared scratch pad for your own devices. Put text, photos or files in on one
device and pick them up on another, a moment later.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. Open scrap.sector809labs.com in your browser and create an account with
your email. You can add it to your home screen if you like. An Android app is
coming soon.

**Can Sector 809 Labs read what I put in it?**
No. Your content is encrypted on your device before it's sent, and only your
devices hold the keys. That also means we can't recover it for you, so keep
your recovery key safe when you create your account.

**What if I forget my password?**
You can reset it with the recovery key you saved when you created your
account. Without it, the only option is to start over with an empty account.

**How big can files be?**
Up to 100 MB each.

**How do I get help?**
Email support@sector809labs.com.

(Keep "Up to 100 MB each" as is: it's the largest file ScrapSheet takes. Don't
add which plan includes it.)
