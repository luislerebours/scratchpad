Delete your account

ScrapSheet, by Sector 809 Labs LLC · Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version. You can delete your ScrapSheet account and everything in it at any time, from the Android app, the website or any device you use ScrapSheet on. It happens right away and can’t be undone. If you pay for Pro, the subscription is cancelled first, so you won’t be charged again.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Open ScrapSheet (the app, or scrap.sector809labs.com) and sign in.
  2. Tap your account button (the circle with your initial, top right), then Account & security.
  3. Tap Delete account…, enter your password, and confirm.

Want a copy first? In the same menu, Download my data saves every sheet, image and file to your device as a .zip.

Uninstalling the app doesn’t delete your account or cancel Pro. Deleting the account does both.

Can’t sign in?

Email support@sector809labs.com from the email address on the account, with the subject “Delete my ScrapSheet account”. We’ll reply to confirm it’s you, then delete the account and everything in it within 30 days (usually much sooner), cancel any Pro subscription, and email you when it’s done.

Forgot your password and don’t have your recovery key? Forgot password? on the sign-in page also offers Start over, which erases everything in the account at once.

What is deleted, right away

  • Your account: email address, the scrambled form of your sign-in secret, and your encryption keys.
  • Every sheet, image and file, including what’s in Recently Deleted.
  • Every share link you made (they stop working).
  • Your plan and billing status, sign-in times and activity counts.
  • Every device is signed out, and this device forgets your keys and its saved copy of your sheets.

We don’t make separate backups of your content, so nothing is kept anywhere else. Because your content is end-to-end encrypted, we never could read it in any case.

What is kept

  • Payment records. If you paid for Pro, Stripe (on the web) or Google Play (in the Android app) keeps its own record of those payments as tax and accounting law requires. We can’t delete those; their own policies apply.
  • Admin actions on your account. If our staff ever changed something on your account (for example, its plan or storage limit), the security log of that action, which names your email address, is kept so changes stay accountable.
  • Error reports. Technical error reports (never your email or content) are cleared after 30 days.
  • The confirmation email we send when the account is deleted.

Delete only some of your data

  • Delete a file or sheet from its ••• menu. It moves to Recently deleted (account menu) for 30 days, then is deleted for good; you can also delete it for good from there right away.
  • Turn off share links in Files & share links (account menu).
  • Clear a sheet’s text from the sheet’s ••• menu.
