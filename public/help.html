Getting started

What is ScrapSheet?

A scratch pad shared between your own devices. Type, paste a link or a photo, or attach a file on one device, and it’s on your others a moment later.

Can I install it like an app?
  • iPhone and iPad: open ScrapSheet in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome, open the ⋮ menu and tap Install app (or Add to Home screen). An app on Google Play is coming soon.
  • Computer: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
Can I use it on a work computer?

Yes. If your workplace’s network blocks the service ScrapSheet uses for live updates and files, it switches by itself to a slower route that works there: everything still syncs and files still upload and download.

Sheets

How do I rename, reorder or delete a sheet?

Use the ••• menu above the text. On a computer you can also double-click a tab to rename it. To move a tab, drag it; on a phone, touch and hold it first, then drag.

I cleared or overwrote my text by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Right after clearing, tap Undo. Later, open the ••• menu and choose Version history: ScrapSheet keeps earlier versions of each sheet’s text for 30 days (one every 10 minutes while you edit, and always before a big change). Tap Show to look at one and Restore this version to put it back. Restoring can be undone too.

Where do deleted sheets and files go?

To Recently deleted in the account menu, for 30 days. You can restore them from there, or delete them for good. They count toward your storage until they’re gone.

Can I add pictures to the text?

Paste or drop them right into the text. Tap one to resize it.

Files and sharing

How big can files be?

Up to 10 MB each on Free, and up to 100 MB each on Pro. If a file is too big, ScrapSheet tells you before uploading.

How do I rename, move or delete a file?

Open the file’s ••• menu: Rename…, Move to… another sheet, or Delete file…. The trash button next to Download deletes too, and on a phone you can swipe a file to the left. Deleting always asks first, and you can undo it.

My upload stopped when I switched apps.

Uploads pause while ScrapSheet is in the background or offline, and carry on from where they were when you come back. Keep ScrapSheet open until uploads finish: if the page is closed (or your phone closes it to save memory), that upload is lost.

How do share links work?

With Pro, open a file’s ••• menu and choose Share link…, or the sheet’s ••• menu and choose Share sheet…. Anyone with the link can open it, until it expires (after 1, 7 or 30 days) or you turn it off. See and manage your links in Files & share links in the account menu. The key that unlocks a shared item is part of the link, so we never see it.

You can also add a password (Add a password, when you make the link or later). Then the link only opens for people who also have the password: send it separately, say in a text. We can’t recover it for you. You can change or remove the password later: the link stays the same, and people who already have it just need the current password. Only Turn off link stops a link.

Can I download everything at once?

Select files and download them as one .zip, or use Download my data under Account & security for every sheet, image and file.

Syncing and offline

How fast do changes reach my other devices?

A second or two after you stop typing (and at least every few seconds while you keep going). The green dot next to “Synced” means this device is connected.

What happens when I’m offline?

You can still read everything, search and copy, but editing is paused (the status says “Offline · view only”) so two devices can’t overwrite each other. When you reconnect, anything you typed just before is saved and editing picks up again.

Do I need to reload when there’s an update?

No. An open ScrapSheet updates itself at a quiet moment, never while you’re typing or uploading.

Privacy and security

Can Sector 809 Labs read my sheets and files?

No. Everything you put in ScrapSheet (text, images, files and their names) is end-to-end encrypted with AES-256 on your device before it’s sent, and only your devices hold the keys. Your password never leaves your device. It also means we can’t recover your data for you: see the recovery key below. More in the privacy policy.

What is the recovery key, and why does it matter?

A 32-character key shown once when you create your account. It’s the only way back in if you forget your password. Keep it somewhere safe (a password manager, or printed). If you lose it, make a new one under Account & security → Generate new recovery key while you can still sign in.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password?. We email you a code, then you enter your recovery key and choose a new password: everything stays. Without the recovery key, the only option is to start over with an empty account, because nobody (us included) can unlock the old data.

How do I change my password or email?

Account & security → Change password… or Change email…. Both need your current password; a new email also needs a code we send to it. Your sheets, files and recovery key stay as they are, and your other devices are signed out.

I think someone else is signed in.

Open Account & security → Signed-in devices… and sign out any device you don’t recognise (or Sign out of all devices), then change your password. We also email you whenever your password or email changes, a new recovery key is made, or every device is signed out.

Plans and billing

What do Free and Pro include?
  • Free: 1 sheet, 1 file up to 10 MB, 10 MB of storage, on all your devices.
  • Pro: 10 sheets, 5 GB of storage, files up to 100 MB each, and share links. $9.99 a year or $0.99 a month.
How do I upgrade, change or cancel Pro?

Upgrade from the account menu. To switch between monthly and yearly, update your card, see invoices or cancel, choose Manage billing. If you bought Pro in the Android app, it’s managed in the Google Play Store. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens to my things if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. You can still open, download and delete everything; Free’s limits only apply to adding more.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

Account & security → Delete account…, then enter your password. It cancels a paying Pro subscription and erases every sheet, file, image and share link, on all your devices. It can’t be undone, so download your data first if you want a copy. Can’t sign in? See how to delete your account.

How do I contact support?

Email support@sector809labs.com, ideally from your account’s address. We can help with billing and your account, but we can’t see or recover what’s in your sheets.
