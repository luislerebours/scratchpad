Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

The short version. What you put in ScrapSheet (text, images, files, their names and types) is end-to-end encrypted on your device before it leaves it. We can’t read it, and neither can anyone who gets hold of our servers.

We keep only what we need to run your account: your email address, a scrambled form of your sign-in secret, and some basic usage numbers. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

ScrapSheet is run by Sector 809 Labs LLC (“we”, “us”). Questions about this policy or your data: support@sector809labs.com.

What we can’t see

Your sheets’ text and names, pasted images, files, file names and file types are encrypted in your browser with keys that only you hold. Your password never leaves your device: it’s turned into a sign-in secret (which is all we receive) and a separate key that unlocks your data. We store only the encrypted result.

This means we can’t search, read, recover or hand over your content. If you lose both your password and your recovery key, nobody, including us, can get your data back.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in, send sign-in codes, and tell you about important changes to your account.
  • A one-way hash of your sign-in secret (never your password) and your encrypted keys, so you can sign in and unlock your data on new devices.
  • Usage numbers we need to run the service and plans: how much storage you use, how many sheets and files you have and their sizes, when they were added, whether a sheet has anything in it, and which images a sheet uses.
  • Account activity: when the account was created, how many times you’ve signed in, and which days you used ScrapSheet in the last 60 days.
  • Signed-in devices: for each device signed in to your account, the kind of device read from its browser (for example, “Chrome on Windows”), when it signed in and when it was last used, so you can see them and sign one out under Account & security. A device’s entry is removed when it signs out.
  • Your plan and billing status: whether you’re on Free or Pro, and your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers (or, for Pro bought in the Android app, the Google Play purchase, product and status), renewal date and status. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Share links you create: what each link points to, when it expires and how many times it was opened. The key that opens a shared item is in the part of the link after “#”, which browsers never send to us.
  • IP addresses, briefly, to limit how many sign-in codes can be requested (to stop abuse). Our hosting provider also keeps standard technical logs for a short time.
  • Error reports: when something on our servers fails, which part failed and when, kept for 30 days. Never the content of what you sent.

On your device

  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to a year, renewed when you visit.
  • Your encryption key, stored by your browser so that it can’t be read back out, until you sign out.
  • An encrypted copy of your sheets and file lists, so ScrapSheet opens instantly. It’s locked with that same key and deleted when you sign out.
  • Anything you share to ScrapSheet from another app (with the app installed on Android), kept on your device only until you add it to a sheet or discard it. When you add it, it’s locked like everything else before it’s sent.
  • A few settings: light or dark appearance, the last sheet you had open, and similar preferences.

We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies, tracking pixels or third-party fonts.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that run parts of the service for us, and only what they need:

  • Netlify hosts the site and stores your account and encrypted data.
  • Cloudflare runs the live sync that tells your other devices something changed, stores the files you upload (in its R2 storage, already locked on your device, so Cloudflare can’t read them), and runs our domain’s DNS.
  • Resend sends our emails: sign-in codes, and security notices when your password changes, a new recovery key is made, every device is signed out, or the account is deleted. It receives your email address and what the email says, including the time and the kind of device (for example, “Safari on iPhone”, read from your browser and not kept).
  • Stripe handles Pro payments as the merchant of record (through its Managed Payments service and Link). Stripe collects your payment details, billing address and tax information directly, under Stripe’s privacy policy. We receive your subscription status, not your card.
  • Google Play handles Pro bought inside the ScrapSheet Android app. Google collects your payment details through your Google account, under Google’s privacy policy. We receive the purchase and its status (not your card), and check it with Google.

Inside Sector 809 Labs, only staff who run the service can see account information, through admin tools that need their own password and two-step sign-in and that record every change. Those tools show account details (email, plan, billing status, storage used and activity), never your content.

We don’t sell or rent personal data, and we don’t share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, but your content is encrypted and we can’t decrypt it.

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and data are kept until you delete them or your account. Deleting your account erases it and everything in it right away.
  • Deleted sheets and files stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then are deleted for good.
  • Ended share links are cleared from your list after 30 days.
  • Sign-in codes expire after 15 minutes.
  • Stripe and Google keep billing records as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can view, change and delete your content in ScrapSheet at any time, download all of it (every sheet, image and file, unlocked on your device) from Download my data under Account & security, cancel Pro from Manage billing, and delete your account and everything in it from Delete account in the account menu (how to delete your account, including if you can’t sign in). To get a copy of the account information we hold, correct it, or for anything else, email support@sector809labs.com from your account’s address. Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your data protection authority. We’ll respond within 30 days.

Security

Besides end-to-end encryption (AES-256), all traffic uses HTTPS, sessions use secure cookies, and sign-in attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, but our design means a breach of our servers wouldn’t expose your content.

Children

ScrapSheet isn’t meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above, and for significant changes we’ll let you know in the app or by email before they take effect.
