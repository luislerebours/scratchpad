Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Scratch Pad keeps your notes and files so you can reach them from your own devices. We don’t show ads, we don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t use tracking or analytics tools.

What we keep

  • Your email address, to identify your account and send sign-in codes.
  • Your password, stored only as a salted scrypt hash. We never keep it in readable form.
  • What you put in Scratch Pad: the text of your sheets and their names, images you paste into them, and files you upload, including small thumbnails of image files.
  • Share links you create: what each one shares, when it expires, and how many times it was opened.
  • Account activity: when the account was made, how many times and when you last signed in, when you last opened Scratch Pad, the days you opened it (the last 60), and how much storage you use. The account’s plan (Free or Pro) is kept too.
  • Limits on repeated attempts: to stop abuse, we briefly keep the times of recent sign-in attempts and code requests, including your device’s IP address for code requests. These expire after an hour or less.

What stays on your device

One cookie keeps you signed in for up to a year, renewed each time you open Scratch Pad. Your browser also remembers which sheet you used last and whether you dismissed a reminder. Signing out or deleting your account removes the cookie.

Who can see your content

Your sheets and files are private to your account. Nobody else can see them unless you create a share link: anyone who has that link can view and download what it shares until it expires or you turn it off. Share link pages are marked so search engines don’t list them.

Your content is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), but it isn’t end-to-end encrypted. The people who run Scratch Pad could technically access stored content; we only would to keep the service running, to help when you ask us to, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your content stays until you delete it.
  • Deleted sheets and files stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days so you can restore them, then they’re deleted for good. You can delete them sooner from there.
  • Ended share links are removed from your list about a month after they end.
  • Our hosting providers keep their own request logs for a limited time.

Deleting your account

In Scratch Pad, tap your initial in the top corner, then Delete account, and enter your password. This erases your sheets, files, images, Recently Deleted and share links right away, and signs you out on every device. You can also ask us to delete it by emailing the address below.

Your choices

You can see, change, download and delete your content in Scratch Pad at any time. If you’d like a copy of the account information we keep about you, or want it corrected or deleted, email us and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Children

Scratch Pad isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly keep information about them. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your content is used, we’ll tell you in Scratch Pad before it takes effect.
