Help & support

Scratch Pad keeps notes, pasted images and files in step across your phone and computers. Here are answers to common questions.

Signing in

I didn’t get the code email

Codes come from Scratch Pad within a minute and look like an animal and four digits (for example, Otter4821). Check your spam or junk folder. You can ask for up to 5 codes an hour; each works once and expires after 15 minutes.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?. We email you a code, then you choose a new password. Resetting signs you out on your other devices.

Sign out of a lost device

Tap your initial in the top corner, then Sign out everywhere. Every device will need your password again.

Using Scratch Pad

Sheets

Each sheet holds text, pasted images and files. Use the ••• menu beside Copy to rename, share, clear or delete a sheet. On a computer you can also double-click a tab to rename it.

Syncing

Changes appear on your other devices within about a second. The dot in the top corner is green when you’re connected. If it says Offline, your changes are kept on screen and saved when the connection returns.

Search

Tap the magnifying glass to search the text and file names in all your sheets. On a computer, press Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac) or /.

Deleted something by mistake?

Deleted sheets and files go to Recently Deleted (tap your initial) for 30 days. Tap Restore to bring them back.

Share links

Share a file or a whole sheet from its ••• menu. Anyone with the link can view and download it, without an account, until it expires (1, 7 or 30 days). Turn links off any time from Shared links.

Your account

Delete my account

Tap your initial, then Delete account, and enter your password. Everything in the account is erased right away and you’re signed out on every device. This can’t be undone.
